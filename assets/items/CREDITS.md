# Items

- `potion.png`: from "CC0 Potion Icons" on OpenGameArt (https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-potion-icons), ArlanTR's set (the blue one) - CC0 1.0
- `heart.png` (no longer used), `tank-full.png`, `tank-empty.png` (no longer used): from "Health bar and heart icon" on OpenGameArt (https://opengameart.org/content/health-bar-and-heart-icon) - CC0 1.0; the full bar turned from green to water blue, the empty bar's red streaks painted out
- `heart-iso.png`, `potion-iso.png` and their `-shadow.png`: made for this game - 3D models (Tripo) drawn at the game's angle as pixel art with spritecast; the potion's glass made see-through by hand
- `heart-hud.png`: the same 3D heart drawn from the front for her hearts at the top, with spritecast
- `tank-cap-l.png`, `tank-cap-r.png`, `tank-glass.png`: the water tank - a glass tube with brass caps (Tripo) drawn from the front as pixel art with spritecast, cut into its caps and one column of glass; the water is drawn in code
